Farm Hands

Click or tap a plot to clear weeds, cultivate the seedbed, or drill winter wheat in September and October. Drag to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Use WASD to move the camera across the farm, arrow keys to select a plot, and Enter / Space to work the soil. Click the farmhouse or press C / H to open the farming calendar. Press Esc for the menu, credits, and keybind settings.
