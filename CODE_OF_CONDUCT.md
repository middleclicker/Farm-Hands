# Code of conduct

Farm Hands welcomes thoughtful discussion and contributions. Treat other people with respect, including when disagreeing about a design or bug. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion, or restrict participation to keep the project safe. They will consider the context and severity of a report and avoid sharing a reporter's identity beyond what is necessary to address it.

Report a conduct concern privately through the [repository's private report form](https://github.com/middleclicker/Farm-Hands/security/advisories/new). For abuse on GitHub itself, you can also use [GitHub's abuse report](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
